Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our movies, series and website.

How can I find a movie?

You can visit the Movies page from the navigation bar to browse the available movies and their information.

How can I watch a movie?

Open the movie details page and click the Watch Trailer or Watch Full Movie button when an available full movie is provided.

What does the movie rating mean?

The rating represents the score given to a movie by viewers and can help users compare different movies.

How can I find a specific genre?

Browse the Movies page and check the genre shown on each movie card to find movies that match your interests.

How can I find information about a movie?

Click View Details on a movie card to see information such as the release year, genre, rating, director, cast and description.

How can I find a TV series?

Go to the Series page from the navigation bar to browse the available TV series.

What information is available about a series?

Each series includes its title, release year, genre, rating, number of seasons and main cast.

Can I add movies and series to my Watchlist?

Yes. The Watchlist page contains movies and series that users can save for easier access later.

Does the website stream movies and series?

No. The website mainly provides information about movies and series and links to available videos.

Can I find both movies and series on the website?

Yes. The website has separate Movies and Series pages where users can browse both types of content.
